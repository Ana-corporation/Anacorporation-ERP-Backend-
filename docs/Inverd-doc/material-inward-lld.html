ERP INVENTORY · MATERIAL PURCHASE ORDERS ONLY

Material Inward — UI, tables, and API

This module records what arrived, what passed quality, what was rejected, what the supplier did about it, where accepted stock was stored, and what finance can invoice. Stock changes only through an inventory ledger row that points at a document.

1. The answer

8UI modules users open
20screens inside those modules
10server API modules
47tables, including shared engines
How a screen connects to the database. The browser calls one module API. That API writes its own document tables, writes a status/activity/task row in the shared platform, and, when stock must move, asks the inventory API to insert a ledger row and update the balance. The screen never updates an item stock column.

Accepted

Stays in QC hold at the receiving dock until put-away. Put-away moves it to a bin as available stock.

Partial

Accepted quantity follows put-away. Rejected quantity stays on the same GRN and opens a supplier case.

Rejected

Moves to quarantine. Buyer splits it across replacement, return, credit, rework, use-as-is, or scrap.

3. Eight UI modules

Module 1 — My Work

Home for every role. It reads open tasks. It does not post stock.

Pending examples: entry drafts, QC queue, supplier cases, overdue replacements, put-away tasks, invoices waiting for a match.

Module 2 — Receiving

Entry checker selects a material PO, sees outstanding quantity, and records the delivery. Submit creates the GRN, posts QC-hold stock, and opens a QC task when the item requires inspection.

Also owns: short receipt (outstanding stays on the PO) and excess receipt (tolerance, warning, buyer approval).

Module 3 — Quality

Inspector records sample size, checklist results, accepted quantity, rejected quantity, and conditional quantity. Those three quantities are separate columns. A partial result creates both a put-away task and a supplier case.

Module 4 — Supplier resolution

Buyer works the rejected quantity. One rejection can be split: 7 replacement + 2 return + 1 credit. Return is its own posted document. Replacement creates a linked GRN, then QC runs again.

Module 5 — Put-away

Storekeeper confirms item, batch or serial, quantity, and bin. The system can suggest a bin from the item rule. Confirm posts the ledger move from QC hold at the dock to available at the bin.

Module 6 — Stock and trace

Balance by warehouse, location, batch, serial, owner, and stock status. Ledger shows every in and out. Document chain lets the user walk PO → GRN → QC → case → replacement or return → put-away → invoice.

Module 7 — Invoice match

Finance matches supplier invoice lines to PO lines and GRN lines. The match quantity basis comes from company policy: accepted, received, or put-away. The screen shows quantity, price, and tax differences, plus GRN accepted and not yet invoiced.

Module 8 — Setup and control

Item flags (QC, batch, serial, expiry), units of measure, warehouse locations, QC templates, rejection reasons, resolution types, over-receipt tolerance, notification hours, inventory periods, document numbers, and role actions.

7. Quantity rules

Each quantity has its own column. A later step adds its own number. It does not replace received quantity with accepted quantity.

PO outstanding

outstanding_qty = order_qty − received_qty − short_closed_qty

received_qty on the PO line sums normal GRNs only. A replacement GRN fulfills a rejected quantity and does not increase PO received quantity, so the receipt does not look like a second purchase. Unapproved excess is stored on the GRN line and is excluded from the PO rollup until a buyer approves it.

Short example. Order 100, this receipt 95. Short on the line is 5. Outstanding stays 5. Buyer leaves the PO open, or posts short_closed_qty = 5 and the line can close.

Excess example. Order 100, this receipt 110, tolerance 2%. Allowed without approval is 102. The extra 8 waits in excess_status = PENDING_APPROVAL. Stock posts for the allowed quantity. The extra posts only after approval.

Partial replacement. Rejected 20. First replacement 12. remaining_qty stays 8 until the second replacement arrives.

Units of measure

The user enters purchase UOM. The line also stores inventory UOM, the conversion factor, and the converted quantity. Example: 1 BOX = 12 PCS. Both the entered 2 BOX and the stock 24 PCS remain on the GRN line. Rounding mode and decimal places come from mst_uom and mst_item_uom.

Stock status versus physical quantity

Physical stock can sit in five states: QC_HOLD, AVAILABLE, QUARANTINE, RESERVED, BLOCKED. Available to issue is only the AVAILABLE balance. Owner type on the same balance row can be company, supplier, customer, consignment, or third party. Version 1 can post company-owned only. The column is already there.

What the chain answers. Arrived 100 on GRN-000421. Accepted 92 plus replacement 5. Rejected 8 because of damage. 2 returned, 1 credited and scrapped, 5 replaced on GRN-000488, rechecked, and stored in bin B05. Available stock is 97. Invoice match uses policy. If policy is accepted quantity, finance can match 97. A supplier invoice for 100 shows a quantity difference of 3. An invoice price of 105 against PO price 100 shows a price variance of 5. GRN accepted quantity with no match row is the GRN-not-invoiced report.

9. Database tables

Every transactional table includes these standard columns: id bigint PK, company_id, created_by, created_at, updated_by, updated_at, row_version int. Document tables also store doc_no where listed. Ledger rows are insert-only: no update of qty, no delete. Corrections insert a reversal row.

12. Stock posting

A status change that keeps the same bin and the same quantity is still two ledger rows when the status changes: quantity out of the old status, quantity in to the new status. Put-away is the same pattern across two locations.

EventOutIn
GRN submitDock · QC_HOLD · received inventory qty, excluding unapproved excess
QC reject portionDock · QC_HOLDQuarantine location · QUARANTINE
QC accept portionNo ledger row. Quantity remains QC_HOLD. A put-away task is created.
Put-away confirmDock · QC_HOLDChosen bin · AVAILABLE
Supplier return postQuarantine · QUARANTINE
ScrapQuarantine or scrap bin · QUARANTINE
Use as isAfter buyer approval, a put-away task moves it the same way as accepted stock
Replacement submitDock · QC_HOLD, linked to the case line
ReverseOpposite quantities, new ledger rows, reversal_of_id filled

Running balance for an item is the sum of qty_in minus qty_out. The balance table is a projection of that ledger, keyed by location and status, so the stock screen does not scan the whole ledger on every open.
